# FileFlipper Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2, 2026_

FileFlipper does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- All file conversions happen locally on your Mac. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
- FileFlipper makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code.
- The only information FileFlipper saves is its own settings (for example, whether it is enabled and
  which folders you allowed it to save into). These stay on your Mac in the app's sandbox container.

FileFlipper is open source, so you can verify all of this in the source code.

Questions: please open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
